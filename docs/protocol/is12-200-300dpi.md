# IS-12 200/300 dpi: explicitly unsupported

The native app represents Canon's documented resolutions but disables 200 and
300 dpi. Neither the app nor eSCL substitutes another acquisition resolution.
FAX remains unavailable because it combines 200 dpi with Canon Text Enhanced
processing. OCR's Text Enhanced processing is also unavailable at 360 dpi.

Evidence checked on 2026-09-14:

* Canon's Macintosh preset table, printed pages 70–72 of the
  [IS-12 manual](https://gdlp01.c-wss.com/gds/2/0900007432/01/BJC85_IS12_user_manual.pdf),
  identifies the requested presets. This is evidence of product behaviour, not
  a wire encoding.
* The saved extended-information response is
  `01 0f 00 81 00 c8 00 b4 82 00 c8 01 68 83 01 2c 01 68`.
  The three apparent tuples are `(81,200,180)`, `(82,200,360)`, `(83,300,360)`.
  Their exact interpretation is unproven. A tuple does not establish square
  200/300 dpi acquisition or imply an interpolation kernel.
* `artifacts/analysis/is12-command-tables.json` pins `BjsInfo.dll` and
  `Bjshidrv.dll` hashes, model 23, and the BJC-85 `s` family. Existing D requests
  use divisors 4, 2 and 1 for 90, 180 and 360 dpi. Static `biSetScanInfo` evidence
  at `BjsInfo!0x10002140` has not yielded a qualified 200/300 dpi path.
* Completed acquisition records exercise 90/180/360. None establishes another
  D parameter, an alternative family, or host resampling for 200/300.

These leave all three possibilities open: different D parameters, a different
mode/family, or host resampling of another native resolution. No bytes were
invented or transmitted to resolve this uncertainty.

Before enabling another mode, recover the original selection path and a golden
command/image fixture, add offline framing and geometry tests, then perform
explicitly scheduled physical content-bearing acceptance. The CLI rejects these
resolutions today; the preset, HTTP validation and settings tests guard the gate.
